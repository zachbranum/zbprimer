# What the reader should do

The job market is as rough as it has ever been right now so the last thing people in the job market need is another hoop to jump through. However technology is always changing, everyone is going to have to adapt whether that be professionally, socially, or personally. It’s important to understand how your messages are being interpreted and the best way to send your message so that your intent is actually what is interpreted. 

I hope everyone on the job market today is becoming aware of the new technology, how it is being used, and how they can use it to their advantage. According to the Oak Ridge Institute, these are the five things that are vital for your resume to pass AI screenings. 1. Read the job application instructions carefully. 2. Keep formatting simple and clean. 3. Use key words from the job description. 4. Consider a resume summary. 5. Write clearly and concisely. With these steps you should not have to worry about AI bias in not being able to interpret your experiences. Another thing noted in the article that is beneficial to consider is that you’re basically writing for two audiences, the AI system as well as the hiring manager that will see it when it gets moved on in the process. So you don’t want your formatting or wording to be too robotic and boring.
