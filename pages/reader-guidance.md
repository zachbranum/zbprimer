# What the reader should do

<p id="guidance-heading">This page translates theory into action. It is the bridge between interpretation and practice for readers facing communication challenges at work, online, and in personal settings.</p>

<h2>Starter guidance structure</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Diagnose the type of communication problem before choosing a response.</li>
  <li>Check both message clarity and meaning assumptions.</li>
  <li>Identify where power shapes whose interpretation dominates.</li>
  <li>Account for algorithmic mediation when communicating through AI tools or platforms.</li>
</ul>

<p>Expand these points with concrete scenarios, decision rules, and audience-specific recommendations.</p>
