# Interpretation and intention

<p id="intention-heading">This page introduces Umberto Eco’s model of intention (<em>intentio auctoris</em>, <em>intentio operis</em>, and <em>intentio lectoris</em>) and extends it for AI contexts through <em>intentio algorithmi</em>.</p>

<p>The new term identifies a practical gap in classical frameworks: algorithmic systems can shape communicative outcomes in ways that neither author nor reader fully intends.</p>

<p>Use this section to define your term clearly, motivate it with examples, and show how it changes interpretive responsibility in AI-driven communication spaces.</p>
