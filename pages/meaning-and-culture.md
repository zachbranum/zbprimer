# Meaning and culture

<p id="meaning-heading">This page introduces a semiotic lens to show how communication creates meaning through signs, conventions, and cultural context. It shifts the focus from transmission efficiency to interpretation dynamics.</p>

<p>Under this lens, the central question is not only whether a message arrived, but what social meanings it activated for different audiences.</p>

<p>Use this page to build your examples, terminology, and core claim about how meaning is produced in AI-mediated communication.</p>
