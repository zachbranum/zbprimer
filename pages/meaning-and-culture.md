# Meaning and culture

In the semiotics model it’s all about signs, symbols, meaning, and understanding. Firstly, AI has a much more difficult time interpreting messages through this model because signs and symbols are a lot less mathematical and analytical than words and direct messages, and more subjective with a variety of possible meanings. When AI evaluates a resume, it interprets these signs according to criteria it was trained on, which may not always be how the recruiter wants that message to be perceived or how the applicant wants that message to be interpreted. Cultural expectations in a professional environment can also influence what certain words, qualifications, and experiences mean and their worth. This means applicants are having to adapt and learn how to communicate with these AI systems so that the signals they want to send are interpreted in the intended way. 

People on the hiring side and AI developers have already recognized this obstacle and have found possible solutions. In the article Forensic Semiotic Recruiting Resume Evaluation and Candidate Engagement Model there is a theory that, “Through natural language processing and LLM we can use a process called forensic semiotics to construct sign systems by which we can deconstruct and interpret candidate resumes and fit them to the model requirements defined by the hiring manager.” With this solution it will make it so much less worrisome for the applicants and hiring managers when thinking about if meaning will be interpreted correctly. This is a huge leap forward to making the AI model more accurate and better at determining which candidates are experienced enough to move on in the process.
