# Interpretation and power

<p id="power-heading">Hall’s encoding/decoding theory is affected by AI in this phenomenon because it interferes with the power dynamic between the sender and the receiver being the applicant and the hiring manager. By having the first round of resumes sorted out by the AI system it takes away some of the power that the hiring manager had before as well as changing how applicants will encode the resume they submit. Now when people on the job hunt are sending in their applications they are more so tailoring and adapting their resumes to the AI agent specifically. This can create a gap between how the applicant intends for their resume to be interpreted by AI vs the hiring manager. 

When looking at communication structure in the eyes of Stuart Hall, concoding and decoding a message can be very different when you just interpret the structure rather than social context. In the article A Marxist-Humanist perspective on Stuart Hall’s communication theory it states, “Communication is reduced to the status of a structure. Such an approach misses that communication is a social process that connects humans and establishes and maintains relations between them.” (Fuchs). This quote relates to this phenomenon when AI reduces most of its communication down to just a structure it is programmed to, it can decode messages a different way than a human would.
